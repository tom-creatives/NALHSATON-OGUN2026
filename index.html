MAIN THEME

Why processing and storage decide the industry's fortune

Nigeria's livestock sector produces large volumes at the farm gate, but loses enormous value before it ever reaches a stable market. This year's conference is built around closing that exact gap — and financing the infrastructure to keep it closed.

ABOUT NALHSATON

The professional voice of Nigeria's livestock scientists

The National Association of Livestock Husbandry Scientists and Technologists of Nigeria (NALHSATON) is Nigeria's foremost professional body for livestock scientists and technologists. The association focuses on animal husbandry, research, production, and policy within the agricultural sector.

Established approximately 30 years ago, NALHSATON's National Headquarters is situated at No. 6 Wallace Street, Lokoja, Kogi State, with state chapters spread across the federation.

Association Motto

"Livestock Production for National Growth"

Membership

Members are drawn from Animal Scientists and Technologists across various fields and specialisations within the civil/public service, government agencies, and local government.

Full Members Associate Members Student Members Honorary Members

Aims & Objectives

01

Provide a common forum for matters affecting livestock production in Nigeria.

02

Defend the integrity, promote, protect, and foster the unity and welfare of members.

03

Act as a collective advisory body on national livestock policy formulation, development, and implementation.

04

Regulate and ensure the allocation of work schedules in livestock establishments to qualified scientists and technologists.

05

Cooperate with organisations within and outside Nigeria with similar objectives involving the association.

06

Defend and seek redress over issues and disputes involving the association.

07

Constitute a National Council for Livestock Production.
